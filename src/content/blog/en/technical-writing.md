New portfolio piece: Technical Writing Case Study

I recently localized a 170-page enterprise tender proposal for an Airport Operations Control Center — from English to Indonesian.

It wasn't just translation. It required:
→ Domain research (aviation, AODB, RMS, integration middleware)
→ Terminology management (150+ technical terms, consistent across all chapters)
→ Structural fidelity (preserved proposal hierarchy and cross-references)
→ Quality assurance (back-translation, peer review, consistency checks)
→ Confidentiality handling (systematic sanitization)

The document itself remains confidential, so this piece is a process case study — what I did, how I did it, and what I learned.
